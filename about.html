OUR STORY

Simple Food. Great Taste.

Jalaram nasta House is all about serving delicious, fresh and flavourful snacks that people love.

From traditional Gujarati favourites like Khaman and Vati Dal Khaman to popular street foods like Dabeli, Vada Pav, Chaat, Pani Puri, Samosa and Frankie, there is something for everyone.

Our goal is simple — good food, great taste and a satisfying experience for every customer.
